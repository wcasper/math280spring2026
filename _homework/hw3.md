### Directions
Solve the following problems and type up your solutions.  Your solutions should be provided in one of the following formats (in order of preference)
* typed up in $$\LaTeX$$ and submitted as a PDF on Canvas
* written legibly on blank paper, scanned into a PDF and then uploaded on Canvas

**Problem 1:**

Consider the indexed family of sets

$$\mathcal F = \{A_n: n\in\mathbb Z\}$$

where here for each $$n\in\mathbb{Z}$$

$$A_n = \{x\in\mathbb R: -1/n <  x\leq (n-1)/n\}$$.

* (a) Determine, with proof, the value of $$\bigcup_{n\in\mathbb{Z}} A_n$$
* (b) Determine, with proof, the value of $$\bigcap_{n\in\mathbb{Z}} A_n$$

**Problem 2:**

A subset $$U$$ of $$\mathbb R$$ is called **open** if it has the following property:

- for all $$a\in U$$ there exists a real number $$r>0$$ such that the open interval $$(a-r,a+r)$$ is a subset of $$U$$

Prove that if $$\{U_i: i\in I\}$$ is an indexed family of sets and $$U_i$$ is open for all $$i\in I$$, then the union $$\bigcup_{i\in I} U_i$$ is also open.

**Problem 3:**

Find an example of a set $$A$$ and a relation on $$A$$ with the specified properties

* (a) reflexive and symmetric but not transitive
* (b) reflexive but not symmetric and not transitive
* (c) transitive but not reflexive or symmetric


**Problem 4:**

For each of the following, prove that the relation is an equivalence relation and describe the specified equivalence classes.

* (a) The relation $$R$$ on $$\mathbb{R}$$ given by $$xRy$$ if and only if $$x-y\in\mathbb{Q}$$.  What are the equivalence classes $$[0]$$, $$[1]$$, and $$[\sqrt{2}]$$?
* (b) The relation $$V$$ on $$\mathbb{R}$$ given by $$xRy$$ if and only if $$x=y$$ or $$xy=1$$.  What are the equivalence classes $$[3]$$, $$[-2/3]$$, and $$[0]$$?



**Problem 5:**

Consider the relation $$\vert$$ on the set $$A = \{x\in\mathbb{Z}: x\geq 0\}$$ defined by $$a \vert b$$ if and only if $$a$$ divides $$b$$.

* (a) prove that $$\vert$$ is a partial ordering on $$A$$
* (b) prove that $$A$$ has an infimum and a supremum
* (c) determine the infimum and supremum of $$\{4,6,10,14\}$$
